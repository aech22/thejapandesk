About The Japan Desk

Hi — I'm [your name], and I run The Japan Desk from Japan.

Most English-language sites about Japanese knives, ceramics, fountain pens, the language, or travel are written from the outside looking in. I'm on the inside. I read the kilns', makers', and brands' own Japanese-language pages, I've used a lot of this in daily life, and I translate that into plain, honest buying advice for readers in the US, UK, Australia, and Canada.

What you'll find here

  • Japanese Craft & Goods — knives, ceramics, bonsai tools, fountain pens and more, explained by tradition and use.
  • Learning Japanese — honest comparisons of apps and courses, checked against how the language actually works.
  • Japan Travel — practical, up-to-date planning using current, first-hand local information.

How I keep this honest

I only recommend things I'd be comfortable using myself. My reviews are grounded in specifications, verified customer reviews, and Japanese-language primary sources. Where I haven't personally handled a specific product, I say so. Some links are affiliate links (see the Affiliate Disclosure), but no maker or shop pays for a recommendation.
